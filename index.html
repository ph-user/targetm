Free content design & management

Content

Our aim is to keep residents engaged with the screens every day. We run a content loop of 16 slots, each displayed for 15 seconds.

The loop blends your building's own notices with the latest news and weather — designed, scheduled and kept fresh by us, at no cost to the building.

Through our content partnerships we also feature curated digital art and premium campaigns, adding to the experience of every screen on the network.
